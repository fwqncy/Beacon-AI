@@ §2.1
## 2.1 Booking travel

Book all flights, trains and hotels through the company travel portal (TripDesk) so that bookings are covered by the company's travel insurance and duty-of-care tracking. Bookings made elsewhere are only reimbursed if they were at least 15% cheaper and booked with manager approval.

Economy class is standard for flights under 6 hours. Premium economy is allowed for flights over 6 hours. Standard class is used for trains.

@@ §2.3
## 2.3 Meals and daily allowances

When travelling overnight for work, you can claim actual meal costs up to £60 per day in the UK, or the country rate published in TripDesk abroad. Alcohol is not reimbursed, except at client entertainment approved in advance by a director.

@@ §4.1
## 4.1 Submitting expense claims

Submit expense claims in the expenses app (Ledgerly) within 30 days of the expense. Claims submitted after 90 days will not be paid. Claims are approved by your manager and paid with the next payroll run after approval.

@@ §4.3
## 4.3 Receipts

Every claim needs an itemised receipt photographed in Ledgerly. Card-machine slips are not accepted as receipts. If a receipt is lost, complete a missing-receipt declaration in Ledgerly; more than two missing-receipt declarations in a year are reviewed by Finance.

@@ §5.1
## 5.1 Company cards

Company cards are issued to employees who travel more than six times per year or who manage supplier payments. Card transactions must be reconciled in Ledgerly within 14 days, with a receipt for each transaction. Company cards must never be used for personal purchases.
