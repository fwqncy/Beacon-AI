@@ §1.2
## 1.2 Everyone's responsibilities

Every employee and contractor is responsible for protecting company and customer information. Complete the security awareness training within your first two weeks and once a year after that. Lock your screen whenever you step away from your device.

@@ §3.2
## 3.2 Data classification

Company information is classified into four levels:

- Public: approved for anyone, such as the website and press releases.
- Internal: for employees only, such as this policy and the org chart.
- Confidential: limited to people with a business need, such as contracts, salaries and financial forecasts.
- Restricted: customer personal data, credentials and security keys. Restricted data may only be stored in approved systems and must never be pasted into chat tools, personal email or unapproved AI tools.

@@ §4.1
## 4.1 Sharing files externally

Share files with people outside the company only from the company drive, using named-person links with an expiry date. "Anyone with the link" sharing is disabled for Confidential and Restricted files. Never send Restricted data by email; use the secure transfer tool.

@@ §5.1
## 5.1 Reporting security incidents

Report any suspected security incident immediately in the #security-incidents Slack channel or by emailing security@company.example. Incidents include phishing emails you clicked, lost devices, data sent to the wrong person and suspicious account activity.

Report even if you're unsure — there is no penalty for a false alarm. Do not try to investigate yourself, and do not delete suspicious emails; the Security team will collect them. Incidents involving personal data must be assessed within 24 hours, because some must be reported to the regulator within 72 hours.

@@ §5.3
## 5.3 Phishing

Forward suspected phishing emails using the "Report phish" button in your mail client. If you entered your password on a suspicious site, change it immediately and report the incident under §5.1.
