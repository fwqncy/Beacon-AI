@@ §1
## 1. Spend approval limits

- Up to £500: line manager.
- £500 to £5,000: budget owner (usually the department head).
- £5,000 to £50,000: budget owner and the Finance Director.
- Over £50,000: Finance Director and the CFO. Over £250,000 also needs the CEO.

Splitting a purchase into smaller requests to stay under a limit is a breach of policy.

@@ §2
## 2. Payment runs

Supplier payment runs happen every Tuesday and Thursday. Invoices must be approved in Spendwise by 12:00 the day before a payment run to be included. Standard supplier terms are 30 days from invoice date.

@@ §3
## 3. Month-end close

Month-end close starts on the third working day of each month. Accruals for any goods or services received but not yet invoiced must be submitted to the Finance team by the second working day. Late accruals roll into the following month.
