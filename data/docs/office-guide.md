## Building access

Your access badge is issued at reception on your first day; bring photo ID. Badges open the building from 07:00 to 21:00 on weekdays. Weekend access needs approval from Facilities at least 2 working days in advance. Report a lost badge to reception so it can be deactivated; a replacement costs nothing the first time.

@@ p.5
## Meeting rooms and desks

Desks are not assigned. Book a desk and meeting rooms in the Spaces app. Rooms booked but not checked into within 10 minutes are released automatically. Phone booths are first come, first served for calls up to 30 minutes.

@@ p.7
## Parking and cycling

There are limited parking spaces, allocated to blue badge holders and employees with a documented need; apply through Facilities. Secure bike storage and showers are available on the lower ground floor. The company runs a Cycle to Work scheme through Perkbox.

@@ p.9
## Visitors

Register visitors in the Spaces app at least one day before they arrive. Visitors must be met at reception and accompanied at all times. Contractors working on site for more than a week can request a temporary badge.
