## Working hours

Standard working hours are 37.5 hours per week. Core hours, when everyone should be available for meetings, are 10:00–16:00 in your local time zone, Monday to Friday. Outside core hours you may arrange your day flexibly with your manager.

@@ p.9
## Annual leave

Full-time employees receive 25 days of annual leave per year plus public holidays. Leave increases by one day for each full year of service, up to a maximum of 30 days. Part-time employees receive a pro-rata entitlement.

The leave year runs from 1 January to 31 December. Up to 5 unused days can be carried over to the next year and must be used by 31 March. Book leave in the HR system (Pulse) and get manager approval; for more than 5 consecutive days, give at least 4 weeks' notice.

@@ p.11
## Sickness absence

If you are unwell and can't work, tell your manager before 10:00 on the first day of absence and record it in Pulse. For absences of more than 7 calendar days you need a fit note from a doctor.

Employees receive full pay for up to 20 working days of sickness absence in any rolling 12-month period, then statutory sick pay.

@@ p.14
## Parental leave

Birth parents are entitled to 26 weeks at full pay, followed by up to 26 weeks at statutory pay. Partners and adoptive parents are entitled to 12 weeks at full pay. Parental leave becomes available after 6 months of service.

Tell People Ops about planned parental leave at least 15 weeks before the expected week of childbirth or placement. Up to 10 "keeping in touch" days can be worked during leave.

@@ p.21
## Remote and hybrid work

Most roles are hybrid: employees are expected in the office at least 2 days per week, on days agreed with their team. Fully remote arrangements need approval from the department head and People Ops.

Working abroad is allowed for up to 20 working days per year, subject to manager approval and a check by People Ops for tax and immigration rules. Request it in Pulse at least 3 weeks in advance.
