## Accounts and passwords

Every employee receives a single company account that signs in to email, the IT portal, Slack and all internal tools. Accounts are created by IT once People Ops confirms the start date in the HR system.

Passwords must be at least 14 characters. Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory for every account and must be set up on the first day using the Authenticator app. Never share passwords or MFA codes, including with IT staff — IT will never ask for them.

If you are locked out, use "Forgot password" on the sign-in page. If MFA is unavailable (for example, a lost phone), call the IT Service Desk on extension 4357 (HELP) to verify your identity and reset MFA.

## VPN and remote access

Internal tools are available from anywhere through single sign-on, so the VPN is only needed for legacy systems: the finance ledger, the build farm and the on-premise file shares.

The VPN client (GlobalConnect) is pre-installed on all company laptops. Connect using your company account. Personal devices must not be connected to the VPN.

Public Wi-Fi is fine for SSO-protected tools. Do not disable the laptop firewall or the endpoint protection agent.

## New starter hardware

Hiring managers order equipment for new starters. Raise a hardware request in the IT portal (Requests → Hardware → New starter) at least 10 working days before the start date. Requests raised later than that cannot be guaranteed for day one, and the starter will receive a loan laptop instead.

The standard kit is a 14-inch laptop, charger, headset and a laptop bag. Engineering and Design roles receive a high-spec laptop by default; choose "high-spec" on the request form for anyone else who needs one and add a justification.

Once submitted, the request goes to the hiring manager's manager for approval and is then fulfilled by Procurement (see Procurement policy §3.2 for delivery). Monitors, docks and keyboards for home offices are ordered separately through the home-office equipment request.

## Lost or stolen devices

Report a lost or stolen laptop or phone to the IT Service Desk immediately, and in any case within 24 hours, even outside working hours. IT will remotely lock and, if necessary, wipe the device.

Stolen devices must also be reported to the police; send the crime reference number to IT. A replacement device is issued once the report is complete. Repeated loss through negligence may be raised with your manager.

## Software requests

Approved software is available in Self Service (on macOS) or Company Portal (on Windows) and can be installed without a request.

For anything not listed, raise a software request in the IT portal. Security reviews new software within 5 working days. Paid software needs a cost centre and manager approval. Browser extensions that read page content need a security review before use.
