@@ §1.1
## 1.1 Purpose and scope

This policy covers every purchase of goods and services made on behalf of the company, including hardware, software subscriptions, contractors, events and office supplies. It does not cover personal expenses incurred while travelling, which are covered by the Travel & Expenses policy.

@@ §2.1
## 2.1 How to buy

All purchases go through a purchase request in the procurement tool (Spendwise). Do not sign contracts or order directly from suppliers. Purchases made outside Spendwise will not be paid.

Approval limits are set out in the Finance Approval Matrix. Purchases under £500 need line-manager approval only.

@@ §2.4
## 2.4 Preferred suppliers

Use a preferred supplier wherever one exists for the category; preferred suppliers are listed in Spendwise. Choosing a non-preferred supplier for anything over £5,000 requires three quotes and a written justification attached to the request.

@@ §3.1
## 3.1 IT equipment

All laptops, phones, monitors and peripherals are bought by Procurement on behalf of IT. Employees and managers must not buy IT equipment themselves and claim it on expenses. Hardware requests start in the IT portal (see IT Handbook p.12).

@@ §3.2
## 3.2 Approval and delivery of equipment

Equipment requests are approved by the requester's manager (for new starters, the hiring manager's manager). Once approved, Procurement places the order, normally within 2 working days.

Procurement ships equipment to the office or to the home address on file in the HR system. For new starters, the address on file is the one given in their offer paperwork; make sure People Ops has the correct address before the request is approved. Deliveries to any other address need written approval from IT.

Delivery takes 3–5 working days within the UK and up to 10 working days elsewhere.

@@ §4.2
## 4.2 Software subscriptions

New software subscriptions need a security review (raised in the IT portal) before purchase, regardless of price. Annual subscriptions are preferred over monthly ones. Renewals are reviewed by the budget owner 60 days before the renewal date.
