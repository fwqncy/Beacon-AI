## Private health insurance

All employees are covered by private medical insurance from their first day, and can add a partner and children at no extra cost. Register through the benefits platform (Perkbox) within 30 days of joining; after that you can only add dependants at the annual renewal in April or after a life event.

@@ p.6
## Pension

You are automatically enrolled in the company pension. The company contributes 6% of salary when you contribute at least 4%. You can change your contribution at any time in Perkbox; changes take effect from the next payroll.

@@ p.8
## Learning budget

Every employee has a learning budget of £1,000 per calendar year for courses, conferences, books and certifications related to their role. Unused budget does not roll over. Request spend in Perkbox before booking; your manager approves requests over £250.

@@ p.10
## Wellbeing

The Employee Assistance Programme offers free, confidential counselling 24/7, including up to 6 sessions with a therapist per issue. Every employee also receives a £40 monthly wellbeing allowance that can be spent on gym memberships, fitness classes or wellbeing apps.
